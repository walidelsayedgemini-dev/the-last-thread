# Google Play Store Listing — English

## Title
The Last Thread

## Short description (up to 80 characters)
A noir mystery in Abis and Alexandria. Find clues. Connect the thread.

## Full description
In Abis, Uncle Mahmoud has vanished, leaving behind a red thread and more questions than answers.

Explore village homes, Alexandria streets, and the Qaitbay harbor. Inspect every scene marker, photograph landmarks, listen to witnesses, and present the right evidence at the right moment. Build your case file and connect the red threads before you accuse the wrong person.

**Features**
- Two Egyptian noir investigations with a warm, atmospheric tone.
- Hand-authored 2D scenes with discoverable locations and clues.
- Branching conversations, questioning, and evidence-based confrontations.
- A case board for connecting evidence and exposing contradictions.
- Story mode without time pressure, plus a Cairo-clock realistic mode.
- A travel map between Abis and Alexandria with location-aware ambience.
- Local saves with multiple investigation profiles and optional export/import.
- Accessibility controls for text size, reduced motion, contrast, and audio.

The game works offline after download. Crash-report sharing is optional and OFF by default; no report is sent unless the player opts in and the publisher has configured a reporting endpoint.

**Note:** The game does not require an account, location access, or in-app purchases.
